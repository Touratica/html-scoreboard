# HTML Scoreboard

A scoreboard overlay for korfball matches, showing the teams, score, game clock and current stage of play.

## Language

### Match structure

**Match**:
A single korfball game between a Home team and an Away team, made up of two Halves, possibly followed by Golden Goal.
_Avoid_: Game, fixture

**Half**:
One of the two main parts of a Match, each made up of two Periods.

**Period**:
One of the four segments of regulation play in a Match, two per Half.
_Avoid_: Quarter

**Technical Time-Out (TTO)**:
The mandatory one-minute break between the 1st and 2nd Periods, and between the 3rd and 4th Periods.
_Avoid_: Timeout, break

**Golden Goal**:
Sudden-death play after the second Half, in which the first goal wins the Match.
_Avoid_: GO, extra time, overtime

### Teams and score

**Home** / **Away**:
The two sides of a Match, each with its own name, colour and score.
_Avoid_: Team A/B, left/right

**Game Clock**:
The main timer of the Match, run once per Period and counting either down or up.
_Avoid_: Timer
